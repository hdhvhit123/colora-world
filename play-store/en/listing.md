# Colora World: Google Play store listing (English)

The app's own English uses British spelling ("colour"), so the captions on the screenshots do too. Use this
text as the **en-GB** default listing. For an **en-US** listing, the same text with "color" spelling is below.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
Colora World: Colour & Play
```
(27 characters)

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Colour pictures that come alive, play in 7 worlds and make books. No ads.
```
(73 characters)

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Colora World is a calm, creative colouring app for young children. Every picture a child colours becomes a toy they can play with, put in a world, use in a story and print in a book.

COLOUR
• 168 original pictures in 14 topics: animals, dinosaurs, vehicles, ocean, space, flowers, food, family, jobs, machines, nature, fantasy, home and celebrations
• Tap to fill, or paint with crayon, marker and watercolour
• Rainbow brush, patterns and stickers
• Three levels, from big simple shapes for little hands to detailed pictures
• Undo, redo and automatic saving, so nothing is lost

PLAY
• Your finished picture comes to life as a toy
• Place your toys in 7 worlds: garden, town, ocean, space, dinosaur valley, fantasy land and a party courtyard
• Day and night, cars that drive, friends that wave

CREATE
• 28 short picture stories with simple steps
• Make a friend: mix ears, tails, wings and hats, then colour it
• Colour Lab: mix red, yellow, blue and white to discover new colours
• Make your own picture book and save it as a PDF to print
• 30 idea cards for when you are not sure what to make

FOR GROWN-UPS
• Works fully offline
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no chat
• Pictures stay on the device unless you make a backup or export a file yourself
• Parent area behind an adult check: profiles, play-time option, sound, language, backup and restore, trash with 7-day restore
• Several child profiles on one device
• Export pictures as PNG, books as PDF (A4 or US Letter) and short videos as MP4
• 13 languages that follow the phone's language: English, Tiếng Việt, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português (Brasil), Italiano, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, ไทย and हिन्दी

All pictures, sounds and music were made for Colora World.
```

## en-US variant

App name: `Colora World: Color & Play` (26). Short description:
`Color pictures that come alive, play in 7 worlds and make books. No ads.` (72).
Full description: the text above with "Colour/colour/colouring" written as "Color/color/coloring"
(the in-app English keeps British spelling).

## Category, tags and contact

- App category: **Education** (alternative: Art & Design). Tags to pick in Play Console: Coloring, Drawing, Kids.
- Contact email and website: **to be filled in by the developer** (Play Console requires a real email).
- Privacy policy URL: host `privacy-policy.html` (in this folder) on a public page you control and paste its URL.
