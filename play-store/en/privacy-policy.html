Colora World is a colouring and creative play app for young children. It is built to work offline and to keep everything a child makes on the device.

Summary

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs, no accounts and no chat.
  • The offline edition of the app does not request internet access at all.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

What is stored on the device

DataPurpose
Child profiles (a picture avatar and an optional nickname a grown-up types; no real name is needed)Keep each child's work separate
Pictures, scenes, invented friends, books, story progress and saved coloursThe child's own creations
Settings (language, sound, play-time option, optional parent PIN)Make the app work as the grown-up chose

This data stays in the app's private storage on the device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup so nothing is uploaded silently.

Files you create yourself

Behind the adult check, a grown-up can export a picture (PNG), a book (PDF), a short video (MP4) or a backup file (.colora.zip). These files are saved only where you choose with the Android file picker, or shared with an app you pick. A backup contains your child's creations; it never contains the PIN or purchase data. Please store backups privately.

Purchases (Google Play edition only)

If the Google Play edition offers extra content, purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details. Google's handling of purchases is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Colora World is designed for children together with their parents or carers. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. To delete everything on the device, use "Delete all data" in the app's parent area or uninstall the app.
